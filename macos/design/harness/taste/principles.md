# Taste principles

Kyle's likes and dislikes for Graphium, at most 15 rules. Seeded 2026-10-17 from the 006 brief he confirmed on 2026-09-06 ([brief](../../../../archive/web/docs/planning/ui-redesign-brief.md), sections 2–5); new rules come only through the `design-feedback` skill with an example card in `examples/`.

1. **An instrument, not a dashboard.** Dense, labelled control surfaces with visible shortcuts. Test: every tool shows its key in its help or menu. Sources: brief §2.1; OP-1, Datapoint 3300.
2. **Ink on dark stock, one warm accent.** Near-black and low-chroma greys; amber only for state. Test: see [one-warm-accent](../rules/one-warm-accent.md). Sources: brief §2.2; Osmo asterisk, amber CRT.
3. **Map first; glass floats, the map doesn't.** The map runs edge to edge; controls float over it on system glass in capsules and soft cards, as in Apple Maps; never blocky three-column chrome. Test: see [glass-overlays](../rules/glass-overlays.md). Sources: decision 007; example 2026-10-17-round2-glass.
4. **Numbers are graphics.** Readouts are allowed to be large and monospaced. Test: see [readouts-in-mono](../rules/readouts-in-mono.md). Sources: brief §2.4; Wonktown numerals, Departure Mono.
5. **Weight and resistance in motion.** Fast, decisive, no bounce; resistance near boundaries; Reduce Motion is the same design at zero duration. Test: no spring or overshoot in any animation. Sources: brief §2.5.
6. **Quiet.** The canvas is the product; chrome recedes in a dim room for hours. Test: the largest dark chrome surface is under 12 % luminance. Sources: brief §3, §9 row 1.
7. **Timeless over trendy; the hell-yes test.** A stranger knows from one screenshot that this is Graphium and not a generic tool. Test: Kyle's answer per screenshot. Sources: brief §2.6; AFK brief.
8. **Not interchangeable, not medieval.** Avoid stock shadcn grey-and-blue, Linear/Notion/Raycast sameness, Foundry's parchment-and-leather, Roll20's panel density, and decorative glassmorphism (tinted, gradient or stacked glass; system glass on the control layer is fine, 007). Test: a screenshot can't be mistaken for one of these. Sources: brief §5; decision 007.
9. **Opinions over options.** Graphium makes a call rather than adding a setting for it. Test: a new preference needs a reason the default can't be right. Sources: brief §4 (Saarinen, "Design is a search for the opinions").
