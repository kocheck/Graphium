# 2026-10-17: Round 2 "Glass over Ground" (Sketch concept)

**Source.** Sketch file `designs/graphum-ui-explorations.sketch`, page "Concepts — Round 2 (map-first)", row "Glass over Ground" (Architect and World View frames). Screenshots live in the claude.ai project folder `design/concepts-round2-2026-10-17/` (`glass-1-architect.png`, `glass-2-world-view.png`); they are Graphium renders but stay out of git with the rest of the concept set.

**What Kyle disliked (round 1, same day).** "I don't like how blocky and late 90s this looks UI wise." "Remember we are building a mapping application." He pointed at Apple Maps.

**What Kyle liked.** "I like the glass." He picked it over the warmer Ink Cartography direction and asked that it be "HIG friendly".

**Description.** Dark map edge to edge under the whole window. Floating, translucent grey glass panels with a thin light edge: a 300 pt session panel on the left (radius 20), a search field capsule top centre, a vertical zoom capsule, round compass and layers buttons on the right, a status capsule (zoom, grid, cursor, distance) bottom left, and the drawing tools in a capsule at the bottom centre with the active tool as a filled amber circle. Fog over unexplored rooms is frosted black. The amber accent marks only the active tool, the selection ring, the measure route and the open map in the list. Type is SF Pro and SF Mono only.

**Carried into rules.** Taste 3 (map first, glass floats) and 8 (no decorative glass); card `glass-overlays`. Native chrome mapping in decision 007: the session panel is the native sidebar and inspector, the tools stay in the toolbar.
